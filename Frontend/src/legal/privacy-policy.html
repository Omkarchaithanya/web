Introduction

Learn about how UrbanTree collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you interact with our platform and services.

Effective: Aug 2026

Information We Collect

When you interact with our platform, we may collect the following types of information to better serve your needs and improve our urban air infrastructure offerings:

  • Personal Identification: Name, Email address, and Phone number.
  • Professional Details: Company or Organization name.
  • Project Context: Specific project requirements or consultation details.
  • Technical Data: Device and browser analytics, IP addresses, and cookie data for website optimization.

How We Use Your Information

We process the data we collect primarily to facilitate our services and improve user experience. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Respond promptly to your enquiries and requests.
  • Provide tailored consultations for clean-air infrastructure deployments.
  • Continuously improve our website's functionality and user experience.
  • Share relevant product, service, and environmental impact updates.
  • Analyze website traffic and performance metrics securely.
  • Maintain seamless and supportive customer communication.

Data Protection & Security

Security is paramount. We implement robust, industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, accidental loss, misuse, disclosure, or alteration. Data is stored on secure servers with strict access controls.

Third-Party Services

To provide a seamless experience, our website occasionally integrates with trusted third-party providers. These may include:

  • Google Analytics (for performance insights)
  • Embedded mapping services (for location contexts)
  • Secure cloud hosting infrastructure
  • Enterprise communication and CRM tools

Cookies Policy Integration

UrbanTree utilizes cookies and similar tracking technologies to analyze trends, administer the website, track users' movements around the site, and gather demographic information. For comprehensive details, please refer to our dedicated Cookie Policy.

Your Rights Regarding Your Data

You maintain full control over your personal data. At any time, users may formally request:

  • Access to the specific information we have stored about you.
  • Correction of any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Complete deletion of your information from our active records.
  • Withdrawal of previously granted consent for data processing.

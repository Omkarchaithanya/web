How UrbanTree Works

Nature + Engineering
In Perfect Balance

UrbanTree is designed to operate through a hybrid power infrastructure integrating conventional grid supply with solar energy systems, enabling reliable, energy-efficient, and sustainable operation across diverse urban and outdoor environments. This multi-stage purification system combines biological absorption, smart airflow engineering, and advanced filtration technologies to create breathable environments within urban spaces.

01

Atmospheric Intake System

Smart directional airflow channels collect polluted air efficiently from the surrounding urban environment.

02

Mechanical Filtration Chamber

Captures heavy dust particles using patented centrifugal funnel dust collection technology for maximum mechanical separation.

03

Prefilter Layer

Traps large airborne particles such as dust, lint, pet hair and dander before they reach the main filter stages.

04

Carbon Filter

Removes VOCs, odors, fumes, moisture and gaseous pollutants through advanced activated carbon adsorption.

05

HEPA Filter

Primary defence against airborne contaminants, designed to trap 99.97% of particles as small as 0.3 micron, including PM2.5, PM10 and other pollutants.

06

Bio-Purification Core

Living moss chamber that kills airborne pollutants and improves air quality through natural biological absorption — nature's own filter at work.

07

UV Chamber

UV light acts as a powerful disinfectant, inactivating and killing airborne microorganisms — bacteria, mold spores, and viruses including Covid-19.

08

Clean Air Redistribution

Purified, healthy, and breathable air is redistributed to create localized breathable zones with improved environmental air quality.

09

Smart Sensor Network

Real-time environmental monitoring and performance analytics continuously adapt the system for optimal purification output.

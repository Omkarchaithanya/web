Everything you need to know about UrbanTree and Bio-Mechanical Air Purification technology.

UrbanTree is an advanced bio-mechanical air purification ecosystem developed by SunEx Technologies to improve urban air quality through intelligent filtration, biological purification, and AI-driven environmental monitoring. The system is designed to create healthier breathable Air Pocket environments in outdoor and semi-outdoor urban spaces.

UrbanTree creates localized breathable Air Pocket zones by continuously drawing polluted air through multiple purification stages and redistributing cleaner air within the effective coverage area of the system. This helps improve surrounding air quality and environmental comfort in high-footfall urban environments.

UrbanTree is designed to reduce airborne pollutants including PM2.5, PM10, dust particles, VOCs, smoke, odors, airborne microorganisms, and other environmental contaminants commonly found in urban and industrial environments.

The Bio-Purification Core utilizes a moss-based biological purification chamber that naturally absorbs airborne pollutants while supporting sustainable environmental air treatment.

Unlike conventional indoor air purifiers, UrbanTree is designed as a scalable urban environmental infrastructure solution capable of creating breathable Air Pocket environments in large public, outdoor, and smart-city spaces using AI-driven and bio-mechanical purification technologies.

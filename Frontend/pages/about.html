What is UrbanTree

A Bio-Mechanical
Air Purification
Ecosystem

Urban air pollution and declining air quality have emerged as critical challenges affecting public health, environmental sustainability, and the future of urban living. Rising levels of particulate matter, airborne pollutants, and harmful emissions continue to impact cities worldwide, creating an urgent need for scalable and intelligent clean-air infrastructure.

UrbanTree is an advanced bio-mechanical air purification ecosystem developed by SunEx Technologies to improve urban air quality and support healthier environments through localized breathable Air Pocket zones. Combining multi-stage filtration, biological purification, intelligent airflow engineering, and AI-driven environmental monitoring, the system is designed to reduce airborne pollutants including PM2.5, PM10, VOCs, odors, and harmful microorganisms.

Powered by smart environmental analytics, sustainable purification technologies, and energy-efficient infrastructure, UrbanTree continuously monitors and adapts to changing environmental conditions to optimize purification performance and improve AQI levels in real time.

Powered By

SunEx Technologies

Bangalore, India

A future-focused technology startup founded by three innovators — Umesh H, Sanjeeth M S, and Dr. Raghavendra Deshpande — driven by a shared vision to address one of the country's most pressing environmental challenges: urban air pollution.

Over the past 2.5 years, the founding team has been actively engaged in research, development, and engineering of intelligent air purification technologies. This journey led to the creation of UrbanTree, an advanced bio-mechanical air purification ecosystem designed to support cleaner, smarter, and more sustainable cities.

SunEx Technologies focuses on designing next-generation smart technology solutions, AI-enabled systems, automation platforms, IoT-integrated infrastructure, and sustainability-driven innovations across residential, commercial, industrial, institutional, and governmental sectors.

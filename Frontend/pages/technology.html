Deep Dive

Technology Components

01
Stage 1

Atmospheric Intake System

The Atmospheric Intake System is engineered to efficiently draw polluted air into the purification unit through smart directional airflow channels.

This system serves as the entry point of the purification process by capturing contaminated air from the surrounding environment. Advanced airflow engineering improves intake efficiency while minimizing energy consumption and operational resistance. Designed for outdoor and high-density applications, the intake system enables consistent purification performance even in areas with fluctuating pollution levels and environmental conditions.

02
Stage 2

Mechanical Filtration Chamber

Uses patented centrifugal funnel dust collection technology to separate and capture heavy dust particles and larger contaminants before advanced purification stages.

This stage enhances filtration efficiency by using centrifugal airflow dynamics to remove coarse particulate matter from incoming air. The funnel-based collection mechanism helps reduce filter load, improves system longevity, and supports high-performance operation in dusty and polluted environments.

03
Stage 3

Prefilter Layer

Captures large airborne particles such as dust, lint, pet hair, and dander before they reach the primary filtration components.

Acting as the first protective filtration barrier, the prefilter prevents larger contaminants from entering deeper purification stages. This improves overall system efficiency, extends the lifespan of HEPA and carbon filters, and ensures stable airflow performance.

04
Stage 4

Carbon Filter

Removes harmful gaseous pollutants including VOCs, odors, fumes, and excess moisture to improve air freshness and safety.

Using advanced activated carbon adsorption technology, this filtration layer targets pollutants that traditional particle filters cannot capture. It effectively absorbs volatile organic compounds (VOCs), smoke, industrial fumes, unpleasant odors, and airborne chemical contaminants.

05
Stage 5

HEPA Filter

Acts as the primary defense against airborne contaminants by capturing 99.97% of particles as small as 0.3 microns, including PM2.5 and PM10.

Designed for high-efficiency particulate removal, the HEPA filtration system captures microscopic pollutants such as fine dust, allergens, smoke particles, pollen, and airborne contaminants. Its advanced filtration structure ensures cleaner and safer breathable air while supporting protection against harmful particulate pollution.

06
Stage 6 — Signature

Bio-Purification Core ✦

Utilizes a moss-based chamber to naturally absorb airborne pollutants and improve environmental air quality.

Inspired by natural purification ecosystems, the moss chamber enhances sustainable air treatment through biological pollutant absorption. The living purification layer contributes to reducing airborne contaminants while improving surrounding environmental balance. This eco-integrated solution combines sustainability with smart engineering to create greener, healthier, and visually adaptive urban purification systems.

07
Stage 7

UV Chamber

Uses ultraviolet disinfection technology to inactivate or eliminate airborne microorganisms including bacteria, mold spores, and viruses such as COVID-19.

This advanced sterilization stage improves air hygiene by exposing airborne contaminants to controlled UV light treatment. The UV system disrupts the biological structure of harmful microorganisms, reducing their ability to survive and spread through the air. Designed for continuous purification environments, the chamber strengthens protection in public spaces, transportation hubs, and healthcare facilities.

08
Stage 8

Clean Air Redistribution

Releases purified, healthy, and breathable air back into the environment through optimized airflow circulation.

After completing the multi-stage purification process, the system redistributes clean air evenly to establish breathable Air Pocket zones that improve surrounding environmental conditions and user comfort.

09
Stage 9

Smart Sensor Network

Provides real-time environmental monitoring, air quality analysis, and system performance intelligence.

Integrated smart sensors continuously track pollutant levels, purification efficiency, airflow conditions, and environmental parameters. AI-assisted monitoring and analytics enable intelligent operational control, predictive maintenance, and data-driven environmental management. The connected ecosystem supports smart city integration while delivering transparent and measurable air quality insights.
